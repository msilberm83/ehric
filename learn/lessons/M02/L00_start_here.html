Module 2 — Start here: outline and the problem

Chapter outline

  1. Turn scope into workstreams. Group the purchased items into major workstreams, such as data conversion, interfaces, training, and testing. You produce a list where every purchased item has a home.
  2. Build a work breakdown. For each workstream, name the outcome, deliverables, tasks, owner, approver, dependencies, dates, evidence, and gate. You produce the work breakdown.
  3. Enter the roadmap in the management system. Use the course's 4 stages and 13 module phases, each ending at its gate, and show where they overlap. You produce the preliminary roadmap.
  4. Set milestones as results. Write milestones that describe an approved result, not only a date. You produce a milestone list.
  5. Plan backward from go-live. Agree the rollout approach early: all at once, by location, or by provider group. Check whether review, correction, and dependency time fit before the target date. You produce a recorded rollout approach, a date check and, if needed, a conflict raised to the sponsor.
  6. Label every important date. Mark each as contractual, operational, external, resource-based, estimated, or target. You produce a basis label on each key date.
  7. Map dependencies and the critical path. Write each dependency with its provider, receiver, need date, and effect of delay. Keep the clearinghouse account and each payer enrollment as separate items. You produce a dependency record and at least one critical-path chain.
  8. Assign authority. Name one accountable person for each decision. Ask the clinic to name a provider champion and super users. You produce a responsibility and decision table.
  9. Run the internal handoff. Send each resource team a written request that answers seven questions, then track acceptance. You produce handoff messages and an acknowledgement log.
  10. Set the operating rules. Define meetings, status colors, escalation, change control, staff change communication, and where records belong. You produce a short operating plan.
  11. Check the gate. Confirm the plan is ready for kickoff in Module 3.

You will produce

  • A preliminary roadmap with milestones, gates, a rollout approach, dated and labeled dependencies, and a critical path
  • A responsibility and decision table, including the clinic's champion and super users
  • Internal-handoff messages with an acknowledgement log
  • A communication, status, escalation, record, and change-control plan

Checked off when every item on the Module 2 gate checklist is true and the plan is ready to present at kickoff.

The problem

Cedar Point Internal Medicine wants both locations on its new electronic health record (EHR) in January. The sponsor has already told staff the date. The vendor's integration lead reports that "clearinghouse setup is complete." Nobody has asked the source vendor for a data export date. Nobody has checked whether the signature pads work with the new system. Kickoff is in three weeks. The sponsor wants to show the plan there.

Before you read: What would have to be true for the January date to be credible?

Does "clearinghouse setup is complete" mean Cedar Point can bill its payers? Why or why not?
