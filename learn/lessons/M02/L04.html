Module 2, Lesson 4: Field assignment

Field assignment

Build the preliminary roadmap for the Cedar Point Internal Medicine practice case:

  1. Enter the 13 module phases, grouped into the four stages, each ending at its gate.
  2. Record the rollout approach and who chose it.
  3. Create the workstreams, deliverables, milestones, and gates.
  4. Tie each purchased item to roadmap work.
  5. Add owners, accountable approvers, dependencies, evidence, and dates. List the clearinghouse account and each payer enrollment as separate dependencies.
  6. Identify at least one critical-path sequence.
  7. Label important dates by their basis: contractual, operational, external, resource-based, estimated, or target.
  8. Add the provider champion and super users to the responsibility table, marked "to be named by the clinic" where the case does not name them.
  9. Draft the internal handoff for each required resource team, and record acknowledgements, declined dates, and escalations.
  10. Add the communication (including staff change communication), status, escalation, record, and change-control rules.

Where the case does not give a fact, record it as open and name the role that would know. Use only the practice case. Never place real patient, client, or employer information in course work.

Knowledge check

  1. Why is a milestone different from a task?
  2. What makes a target date credible?
  3. How can the implementer identify a likely critical-path item?
  4. Why is "clearinghouse setup complete" not the same as billing ready?
  5. What must an internal-handoff request contain to be actionable?
  6. Why does QC need the scope and acceptance evidence before configuration is complete?
  7. What information should a material change request analyze?

Write your answers before you look at the model answers at the end of this chapter.

Spaced review

These questions bring back Module 1. Answer them without looking back.

  1. A practice administrator emails you: "Sales promised us a fax interface." The signed order does not list one. Where does the fax interface go in your plan, and who owns the next step?
  2. Your control log says: "The clinic may lose its office manager before go-live." Another entry says: "The order lists one location; the statement of work (SOW) lists two." Classify each entry, and explain how you decided.
  3. While building workstreams, you find an outcome with no success measure: "fewer missed appointments." What do you add, and who owns it?
  4. A resource-map row lists the vendor trainer as both responsible and accountable for staff competence. What is wrong, and how would you fix it?
