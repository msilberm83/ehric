Module 9, Lesson 5: Your turn: faded example

Your turn: faded example

If you have done this task on a real project, try this first, then compare with the worked example.

A clinic's reference-lab results interface uses HL7 v2 result messages. In testing, every message returns an "accepted" acknowledgement.

  1. The tester compared the business result to the transport result. Two results for a synthetic patient with a common name filed to another patient with the same name and a different birth date. Produced: a failed test step. Why: a transport acknowledgement does not prove the result filed to the right chart.
  2. The tester logged a defect. Produced: a defect record linked to the failed step. Why: a wrong-patient result is a safety failure that needs a tracked fix.
  3. Who decides what happens to results that fail the birth-date match? Produced: ________. Why: ________.
  4. What does the vendor change, and what does QC test afterward? Produced: ________. Why: ________.
  5. What must be in place before the interface is approved? Produced: ________. Why: ________.

Field assignment

Use the Cedar Point Internal Medicine practice case (course casebook, Track A, Month 9 packet). Do not use a real organization, project, or data. Select one integration other than the clearinghouse, such as the payment vendor or the electronic-signature service. Also select two configuration areas.

  1. Complete the integration inventory and owner map.
  2. Draw the data flow and security boundaries.
  3. Record contracts, enrollment, credentials, user accounts, environment, and outside dependencies.
  4. Build a prescriber readiness tracker for EPCS for the case's prescribers, with owners and need dates.
  5. Define acknowledgement, error, retry, duplicate, downtime, and reconciliation behavior.
  6. Write at least ten end-to-end tests with the expected result of each.
  7. Build governed form, code, template, or payment records for your two configuration areas.
  8. Draft a source register entry for one clinical exchange the case needs, such as sending a referral note.
  9. List the technical, business, clinical, privacy, security, and financial approvals needed, and who gives each.
  10. Assign operational monitoring and support.

Self-check rubric. Your work is ready when:

Knowledge check

  1. Why is a successful connection not the same as a successful business workflow?
  2. What is the difference between transport acknowledgement and business acceptance?
  3. What does a 999 tell you, and what does a 277CA tell you?
  4. How is a rejection different from a denial, and how is each one worked?
  5. Why must clearinghouse enrollment and each payer's ERA and EFT enrollment be tracked separately?
  6. Why does "supports USCDI" need a version number, and where would you check certification?
  7. Who decides whether a configuration choice could be information blocking?
  8. Why must both endpoints have owners?
  9. What controls prevent forms, codes, and templates from becoming inconsistent?
  10. What must be transferred to operations before an integration is complete?
  11. Which EPCS steps must be done for each prescriber, and who completes them?

Spaced review

  1. (Module 8) A test conversion at a dermatology clinic shows 2,400 source patients and 2,385 loaded. The vendor says the difference is "within tolerance." What must happen before the data owner accepts the run?
  2. (Module 8) A converted medication list has 40 uncoded entries. A physician asks you to delete them so the list looks clean. What would you do?
  3. (Module 6) During provider training on lab orders, a physician asks who will see results that do not match a patient. Why does this question belong in the scenario, and who should answer it?
  4. (Module 5) A medical assistant attended every training session. The practice manager asks you to mark her competent so she can get access. What evidence is needed first?
  5. (Module 2) A prescriber's EPCS identity proofing depends on an outside credential company. How would you write this item in the dependency record?
  6. (Module 1) The practice manager says the salesperson promised a link to the state immunization registry, but the signed order does not list it. How do you record it?
