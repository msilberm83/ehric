Spaced review

These questions come from earlier modules. Answer them without looking back.

  1. (Module 12) On day 12 of stabilization, tickets are low, but nobody has checked the interface error queue for a week. The sponsor wants to end stabilization today. What do you say?
  2. (Module 12) During go-live, a vendor analyst changes a routing setting and posts "fixed" in the team chat. What must happen before this change counts as complete?
  3. (Module 10) A vendor offers to turn on an AI tool that drafts replies to portal messages. Before anything else, what do you write, and what must it name?
  4. (Module 9) A clearinghouse integration passes its 999 and 277CA tests. What else should you test before you call it complete?
  5. (Module 2) The roadmap lists "Training — March 10" as a milestone. Why is this weak, and how would you rewrite it?
  6. (Module 1) For a historical data conversion, name the four roles you record. Which one approves the result?
