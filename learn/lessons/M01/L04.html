Module 1, Lesson 4: Knowledge check

Knowledge check

  1. What does the signed order establish, and what does it not establish by itself?
  2. How should an undocumented sales promise be handled?
  3. Which sources make a commitment documented, and which make it remembered?
  4. What is the difference between an assumption and an issue?
  5. Why is the person responsible for a task not always the person accountable for the result?
  6. Name three conditions that should bring privacy, security, clinical, billing, or other specialist review into the project.
  7. What evidence could show that a purchased workflow is operational rather than merely configured?

Write your answers before you look at the model answers at the end of this chapter.
